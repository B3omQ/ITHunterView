SOFT-SKILL EVIDENCE GUIDANCE
- Self-learning: certifications or side projects are stronger than a broad stack without learning context.
- Teamwork: a stated role and contribution in a team are stronger than the word "team" alone.
- Communication: a concrete stakeholder, presentation, documentation, review, or coordination action is stronger than a generic self-claim.
- Problem-solving: a concrete technical or product challenge and the candidate's action are stronger than generic CRUD work.
